          Backup

          step by step

          How to use it

          1. Journals. Make one journal for each notebook with New journal, and give each its own colour.
          2. Scan. In Scan, pick the journal, then take photos or choose them, from the first page to the last. Claude types each page up and finds its date.
          3. Read. Open a journal to turn through the pages side by side. Use the arrow keys, swipe, or drag the slider at the bottom to jump to a year.
          4. Dates. Pages without a date are shown as between the dated pages around them, so everything stays in order. Add or fix a date by opening the page with Details.
          5. Arrange. In a journal, Arrange lets you drag pages into the right order or move them to another journal.
          6. Timeline. Shows every journal together in date order. Tap the journal names at the top to show or hide them.
          7. For the book. Tap the star on any page to save it for the book. Search, PDFs and text exports can use just those pages.

          Keyboard on the Mac: ← and → turn pages, Home and End jump to the first and last page, T shows the typed text.
