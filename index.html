      Backup

      Your pages are kept only on this phone. Save a backup now and then, and keep it somewhere safe such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. You can also use a backup to move everything to a new phone.

      How to use it

      1. Scan. Lay the page flat in good light. Tap Scan a page and take the photo. Or use Choose photos to pick several pages you already photographed.
      2. Trim. Drag the corners so only the page is left, then tap Save page.
      3. Reading. The phone reads the handwriting by itself. It takes a little while per page and works without internet.
      4. Check. Open a page to fix any words it got wrong, and add a title, date, notebook and tags. Tags like family or chapter 3 make pages easy to find later.
      5. Search. Type words in the search box. Put words in quotes to find an exact phrase, like "summer house". Use Filters to narrow by notebook, year or tag.
      6. PDFs. Open a page and tap Save as PDF, or search and tap Make a PDF of these to get all matching pages in one file.

      Handwriting is read on the phone with free software, one line at a time. It handles cursive far better than ordinary text scanners, but it still makes mistakes, especially on faded or cramped writing. Every word you fix is remembered and fixed automatically on later pages. Search also finds words that are one letter off.
